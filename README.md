# Researchable Alchemy Recipes

Research the alchemy recipes you need at the study table, with ingredient sources included.

A planned mod for **Graveyard Keeper 1.407 on Windows PC**, using **BepInEx 5**.

When an alchemical product becomes known through an available in-game source, it becomes a research topic. Study the product to learn its alternative formulas and how to obtain their ingredients. Choose suitable source materials, complete their required vanilla studies, and manufacture the product through ordinary alchemy.

The alchemy journal will show available research topics, known formulas and ingredient acquisition methods. Research is selected and performed at the study table; the journal is informational.

**Status:** concept selected; design and host-mechanics verification in progress. No playable build is available yet.
