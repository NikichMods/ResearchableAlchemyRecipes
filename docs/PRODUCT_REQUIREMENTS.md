# Product requirements

Status: adopted working concept. Project name and public repository approved on 2026-10-11. Research-only mechanism candidates exist; production implementation is not yet started.

## Outcome

For an alchemical product known to the character, the player can follow a predictable in-game route from missing knowledge to ingredients and manufacture, without mandatory brute force, recipe deduction or an external wiki. The player remains responsible for ordinary material acquisition, vanilla material study, equipment and production.

## Adopted player flow

1. An available, verified in-game source mentions a product. This counts as character awareness even if the player has not opened that source's window.
   An actually given native task asking for an alchemical product is an awareness source immediately. The user adopts this on 2026-10-14: do not make the player first open merchant trade or build a separate source station to research the requested product. In particular, Clotho's initial healing-potion task must offer healing research after the native alchemy access gate opens, before memory restoration/trade. Native already-known formulas still suppress redundant research; the task does not grant a formula automatically. Audit other ordinary and native quest products for the same boundary, including authored disclosure cases.
2. After the character has unlocked the vanilla The Beginning Of Alchemy technology, eligible products appear as research topics. Earlier awareness alone does not open the mod's research/information access. External player knowledge does not reveal the entire catalog.
3. The player selects, pays for and performs research at the study table. Possession of the target product is not required.
4. Research reveals all ordinary alternative formulas for the product and the acquisition methods of their ingredients.
5. The player chooses suitable material sources, performs any required vanilla material studies, produces ingredients and uses ordinary mixed alchemy.

## Knowledge and payment

- Reveal ingredient-source information with product research; no separate paid reagent-source research is required.
- Show all source alternatives within the supported host-data scope, each material, processing method, equipment and required conditions. Include ordinary producer paths where relevant, not just alchemical decomposition. Do not explain world acquisition of the source material itself.
- Show alternative sources without automatically completing their vanilla studies or charging for all of them.
- Respect previously known formulas and material studies, irrespective of whether knowledge came from crafting, vanilla authored disclosure or this mod.
- Recipe research is a single work operation. Quantity/queue extensions must not charge for repeated copies of the same research, and fully known products must not start another paid investigation.
- One product research reveals its remaining formulas. If some are already known, display them immediately and label the remaining topic as studying other manufacturing methods.
- Adopted Russian partial-topic indication: `Часть рецептов известна. Открыть все варианты.` It explicitly states prior knowledge and complete research coverage. Show it only for partially known product groups; all-unknown topics do not need the explanation. This replaces the earlier accepted `Другие способы изготовления`; generic unknown-recipe wording was rejected because it failed to distinguish partial topics.
- Knowledge remains available for repeated reference and across recipes. Ordinary repeat manufacture pays production costs, not another information fee.
- Recipe research charges no faith: the user adopts zero on 2026-10-14 because ordinary material Study remains separately paid. Research uses the adopted science tariff plus native-style energy and manual work. Energy/work should progress smoothly from the cheapest ordinary material Study endpoints for the 1-science group to the most expensive endpoints for the 10-science group. RESEARCH_PRICE_REVIEW.md owns verified endpoint values and the proposed rounded interpolation; exact intermediate values await review. Recipe knowledge and guidance are the reward; additional red/green/blue technology-point rewards are not required.
- Adopted working science tariff: 1/2/4/8/10; RESEARCH_PRICE_REVIEW.md owns all 33 assignments. Decorative paints 1 each; black/white paint, acid and healing 2; fertilizer energy elixir 4 (latest reduction from 8, single topic for boost I/II); flavor enhancer 8 (quality II); growth enhancer 4 (quality I); freshener 4 (incense I); aromatizer 8 (incense II); dark elixir 8; golden/silver elixirs 10 each. Fertilizer role names are separate products, not two elixirs jointly required by one quality-II recipe. Approximate early/middle/late groups do not add dynamic prices or new access gates. Faith is fixed at zero; exact energy/work interpolation and partial-knowledge treatment remain under review. Installed diagnostic costs are unchanged.
- Do not price research by the number of alternative formulas. The user rejects that proxy on 2026-10-14: variant multiplicity does not establish product value; secondary/decorative topics should not become expensive merely for offering more methods. Science as the principal fee, a zero/one-faith option, progression-related price groups and partial-knowledge discounts remain under investigation, not adopted replacements for the current resource contract.

## Study-table presentation

- Present ordinary material Study and recipe research as two tabs in a common native-style frame. Tabs use icons, as on vanilla workbenches, rather than permanent text captions. Exact icon choice and final presentation remain subject to visual acceptance.

## Contextual recipe guidance

- Custom visible text follows the game's selected language, covering all 11 native locales with Russian/English prioritized. Item/equipment/NPC names and fonts remain native; unknown locale codes fall back to English. Study marks retain atomic wrapping and source indentation in every language. LOCALIZATION.md owns implementation/evidence limits; translation completeness is separate from installed rendering/editorial acceptance.

- Adopted Russian source wording: "Использование предмета" above the native item name; "Извлечение из тела" / "Сожжение тела" below the applicable equipment; "Покупка" above the native NPC name. Preserve all availability/technology annotations. Avoid duplicating "Тело" and a parenthetical description of the same action.

- Final mod presentation must support all game languages and the complete supported ingredient population. Current research guidance implements all 11 native locales, with bounded installed visual acceptance in 0.1.33. Visual grouping must not depend on Russian names or one representative reagent's colours/line lengths.
- First-version guidance lives with known formula components at the native alchemy tables; a standalone journal/character-window tab is deferred and is not required for the initial feature. Reconsider it later as optional consolidated browsing, not a prerequisite.
- Research starts at the study table. Known formulas remain available through ordinary alchemy-table lists.
- Ingredient guidance must be available for repeated reference while inspecting a known formula: reagent -> source alternatives -> processing method/equipment/conditions -> remaining material-study requirement.
- Recipe information and product research become accessible only after The Beginning Of Alchemy is actually unlocked. Apply the same progression gate to contextual recipe guidance and the research entry at the study table. A pre-unlock locked entry is still an open presentation choice.
- Product entries use native item icons and names; a known product shows its known alternative formulas and ingredient composition. Partial knowledge must not reveal still-unknown variants before the remaining product research.
- Product -> ingredient -> acquisition methods supplies the relevant material information; an unrelated all-material encyclopedia is not required.
- Display remaining vanilla material-study requirements clearly and account for completed studies.
- For a source blocked by native material Study, identify the material and make the next action clear: study that source at the study table before processing. Do not automatically study it as part of recipe research.
- Use a compact "не изучено" marker on each affected source route and one conditional explanation below the block. Adopted Russian prototype wording: "Отмеченные материалы нужно сначала изучить на исследовательском столе." This refers to the selected marked source route; it does not require studying every alternative. Keep source/equipment/marker together on one line when the available width permits; first use the available width without shrinking the native font. Final long-row behavior remains subject to visual evaluation.
- Ingredient section names are headings with readable hues associated with their icons. The user accepts B, then selects light C without outlines for life powder/fluid/essence, white/silver/gold powders, salt and oil. All other headings retain B. REAGENT_HEADING_PALETTE.md owns exact mappings and acceptance status; stable item IDs own colours. Ordinary source alternatives remain neutral and pending-source marks amber.
- Failed-experiment alchemy goo sources remain readable but visually secondary at 35% opacity, ordered after ordinary materials within each equipment list. Subdue the complete row including its Study/condition annotation. A section containing only goo also has its equipment heading subdued and follows ordinary sections. No equipment is always relegated to the bottom independently of its sources. Monster jelly is not failed-experiment goo and retains ordinary emphasis. No source recipe or native study state changes.
- Preserve the native recipe card/window geometry, ingredient/energy positions, navigation and quantity controls. Display source guidance separately beside the window in the familiar native tooltip visual style, immediately following mouse hover or gamepad focus. Inline expanding cards and enlarging the shared alchemy window are rejected after wider usability testing.
- The next visual prototype uses height determined by text content, with no scrolling implementation yet. A competing workbench-style panel and two-sided comparison are dropped; native-tooltip appearance is chosen. Exact placement, fit and input behavior still require inspection and usability verification.
- Do not add an "any quality" note. Show a specific quality only where genuinely required. The one-time verified current source corpus has no such requirement, so show plain crop-family names without per-display quality filtering; no quality-policy subsystem is required now.
- Anchor the tooltip beside the native window's lower-right corner, growing upward with measured clearance for quantity arrows. Use a rectangular tooltip without a tail. Avoid revealing intermediate layout/position passes; eliminating the visible jump takes priority over retaining an extra clearance correction. Add separation between product title and source-information body; use readable, visibly coloured foregrounds for parchment.
- Use a narrow vertical layout with equipment subheadings and individual material rows. Preserve extraction/burning/inventory-use explanations for now; their removal is not adopted. Compatible combined equipment levels use Roman digits and comma-space, e.g. `I, II`.
- Latest visual feedback prioritizes immediately scannable reagent/equipment/material indentation over maximum narrowness. Do not split study-marker words/brackets or quantities into orphan fragments. The user requests Roman equipment levels (e.g. `I, II`), superseding the preceding numeric presentation choice. English and Russian are the first localization/acceptance targets; preserve the same structure for all native game languages, using current-language font/text measurements rather than Russian fixed lengths. This does not authorize or require full translation implementation in this visual iteration.
- Reduce repeated equipment names by equipment subheadings and material lists. Preserve each source's native study/availability conditions; combine equipment tiers only where their applicable conditions/results are compatible. Omit numerical input/output amounts from this acquisition guide: scanning source alternatives takes priority over production-efficiency detail. This changes presentation only, not native yields or recipe inputs. Omit DLC-exclusive source alternatives when the native DLC availability check says that DLC is absent.
- Keep the localized study marker as text for the next candidate; do not introduce a new icon/legend yet. Measure full source rows including the marker when choosing width. If screen space forces wrapping, preserve the material-row indentation on every continuation and keep the entire marker together. The shared study explanation may wrap normally.
- Minimum source-coverage acceptance: every ingredient of every ordinary/native quest formula shown at either manual alchemy table must have at least one confirmed useful acquisition route that does not rely on slime. Retain slime alternatives in the tooltip, but they do not satisfy this coverage criterion. Check the complete native corpus, not only formulas currently known on the user's save. Preserve quest/technology/DLC availability annotations; a confirmed route is not necessarily usable at the current progress stage. Honest unknown-source fallback is a diagnostic for uncovered/new content, not an acceptable completed ordinary-corpus entry. This criterion does not demand an exhaustive catalogue of every possible acquisition method or change paid-research eligibility.

## Existing saves and invariants

- Installing the mod after progression must not require restarting or replaying technology unlocks merely to register research topics. Reconstruct current eligibility and knowledge from verified save-backed/native state on load.
- Read the existing technology unlock state on old saves; opening access must not depend solely on observing a new Clotho conversation or a future unlock event.
- Awareness of a past disappeared item or interaction cannot be promised unless a persistent host record is established. Current-source eligibility is the selected rule.
- For resurrection liquid, the user selects the opened vanilla resurrection technology (`Second chance`) as the awareness source. Its persistent native unlocked_techs record is sufficient without a built resurrection table, body, liquid sample or prior window visit. Keep The Beginning Of Alchemy access and remaining-formula gates. This is a specific technology source, not a rule revealing products from every unlocked technology.
- Temporary crafting activity at an otherwise accessible church workbench does not hide research topics derived from its unlocked recipes. User adopts this on 2026-10-14. Preserve physical presence/removal/disable/tutorial and native recipe visibility gates; target-input ownership and station idle state are not awareness prerequisites.
- Goods on locked merchant tiers do not establish product awareness. The user treats dim unnamed icons behind the native tier overlay as unrecognizable, even if item cells and stock records exist. Only unlocked trade tiers may contribute merchant-source topics; this does not waive trade access, current source or remaining-knowledge gates.
- For Clotho potion discovery, the player must actually open native trade. That opening introduces all currently stocked potions on unlocked tiers together, without scrolling/hover/purchase. Opening a higher tier alone does not introduce its goods; revisit trade after the upgrade. Record per-product discovery in namespaced native player Item parameters, scoped to that save; keep current trade/physical/tier/stock and remaining-knowledge gates. User adopts this on 2026-10-14, correcting the previous narrative-only rule. Native @clotho_trade unlock establishes access, not discovery. Old saves deliberately need one new trade opening because no reliable native prior-visit record was found; do not distinguish or guess save age. This is the adopted bounded exception to native-only awareness reconstruction/no-replay. No separate file or general encounter journal is required.
- Preserve vanilla source-material study and ordinary manufacturing. Unknown formulas are not automatically revealed just because the product becomes a research topic.
- Targeted recipe purchasing from Clotho is a separate possible future mod, outside initial scope.
- No logic minigame, deduction requirement, generator or UI is inherited from AlchemyRiddle.

## Deferred research-availability notifications

- The user wants a player-visible notification when new research topics become available, identifying the products together. Opening Clotho trade is a representative event; the broader source families must be considered too. Do not require a study-table visit merely to learn that new topics exist.
- Defer this work until ordinary recipe source coverage is resolved. Prefer an appropriate native-style popup after its host owner is verified; no notification mechanism or hook is selected yet.
- Coordinate timing with trading, technology/reward dialogs, native popups and tooltips. Notifications must not overlap/disrupt an existing window; choose a safe queue/delivery boundary and aggregate/deduplicate related new topics. Exact presentation, timing and old-save/initial-load behavior remain design choices.
- Verify source availability transitions and window conflicts using bounded replayable/offline event fixtures and targeted native probes where needed. Do not require the user to replay every quest/unlock naturally. Distinguish such simulated checks from real native window/lifecycle acceptance.
## Acceptance envelope

A representative unknown product can be researched without owning it; the native alchemy-table list shows usable formulas and contextual component guidance supplies their acquisition routes. A chosen unstudied source is explicitly identified as needing vanilla study; unrelated alternatives remain unstudied and unpaid. Previously known formulas/materials are recognized. Loading an existing save yields correct current topics. Repeated manufacture and repeated information viewing do not charge another knowledge fee. Save reload preserves the resulting knowledge. Exact research economics and interface acceptance will be defined before the corresponding candidate.
