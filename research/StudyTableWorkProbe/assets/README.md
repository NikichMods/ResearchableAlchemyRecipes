# Original tab artwork

SPDX-License-Identifier: MPL-2.0

These two original software-supporting PNG assets are licensed under MPL-2.0 as part of this project. They were authored and visually selected in the project artwork chat, including the user-edited Material Study composition; they are not extracted Graveyard Keeper sprites. Private drafts and references are excluded.

Both files are unchanged copies of local final-tab-icons-v1, RGBA 40x26, seven opaque grey-blue colours, binary alpha. Keep full canvases and transparent margins intact for shared magnifier alignment.

- MaterialStudy-icon-40x26.png: leaf, material fragment and butterfly under a light magnifier. SHA-256 a4fc4f332558ab283f7972d1c8a4ed64cb7273fd55e01368221f027ae9181e2b. Opaque bounds (9,3)-(37,25), right/bottom exclusive.
- RecipeResearch-icon-40x26.png: parchment with flask diagram under the same magnifier. SHA-256 7675fedcec0c3aba2f558cbda3f6f6980ab3e62653d412be0045a73281ce02ff. Opaque bounds (5,2)-(36,25), right/bottom exclusive.

These are embedded in the research DLL as RAR.MaterialStudy.png and RAR.RecipeResearch.png. A successful byte-preserving embedding check does not establish native rendering/tint acceptance.
